Biography

Hi! I am Yitao Liu (刘奕焘), currently a Ph.D. student at the School of Engineering, Westlake University, majoring in Sustainable Development Science and Technology under the supervision of Prof. Liang Lei (雷亮).

I received my B.S. in Geological Engineering from China University of Geosciences (Wuhan). During my undergraduate study, I ranked 2/69 with a GPA of 3.93, and also completed a minor in Computer Science and Technology. In 2025, I received a full scholarship from the China Scholarship Council (CSC) for an exchange at Peter the Great St. Petersburg Polytechnic University.

My undergraduate research focused on geotechnical engineering, granular materials, slope stability, rock-soil mechanics, and numerical simulation of geological hazards. I am especially interested in combining experiments, mechanics, numerical methods and data analysis to understand complex geomaterials and hazard processes.

Feel free to contact me if you are interested in discussing or collaborating with me!
